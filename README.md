# PayBridge

## Overview

PayBridge is a secure escrow payment platform that bridges the trust gap between buyers and sellers in peer-to-peer and online transactions.

It ensures that funds are safely held in escrow and released only when agreed transaction conditions are met, protecting both buyers and sellers from fraud, non-delivery, and payment disputes.

---

## Problem Statement

Online and peer-to-peer transactions often suffer from trust issues such as:

* Buyers paying without receiving goods or services
* Sellers delivering without guaranteed payment
* Lack of structured dispute resolution
* Difficulty proving delivery or fulfillment
* Transactions happening outside secure systems

PayBridge solves these issues by introducing a structured escrow-based transaction system.

---

## Solution

PayBridge provides a secure transaction layer that:

* Holds funds securely in escrow
* Releases funds only after delivery confirmation
* Enables seller-initiated invoice transactions
* Supports buyer authentication (login or guest checkout)
* Provides proof-of-delivery tracking
* Handles disputes through structured workflows

## System Workflow

### 1. Seller-Initiated Transaction Flow

1. Seller creates an invoice on PayBridge
2. System generates a unique invoice link
3. Seller shares invoice link with buyer (WhatsApp, email, etc.)
4. Buyer opens the invoice link
5. Buyer proceeds to checkout flow

---

### 2. Buyer Checkout Flow

Upon opening the invoice link, the buyer can:

* Create a new account
* Log in if already registered
* Continue as guest (guest checkout allowed for first interaction)

After authentication or guest access:

1. Buyer reviews transaction details
2. Buyer confirms payment
3. Funds are sent into escrow
4. Transaction becomes active

---

## 🚚 Delivery Flow (Escrow Lifecycle)

Once payment is successfully made and funds are held in escrow, the transaction enters a structured delivery lifecycle with real-time status tracking for both buyer and seller.

---

### 🔵 Step 1: Payment Confirmation

1. Buyer completes payment via invoice link
2. Funds are secured in PayBridge escrow account
3. Seller receives payment notification
4. Transaction status updates to:

```text id="status1"
FUNDS IN ESCROW
```

---

### 🚚 Step 2: Seller Marks Item as In Transit

After preparing the item for delivery:

1. Seller clicks **“Mark as In Transit”**
2. System updates transaction status to:

```text id="status2"
IN TRANSIT
```

3. Both buyer and seller dashboards reflect:

* Item is on its way
* Timestamp of dispatch
* Seller delivery notes (optional)

---

### 📍 Step 3: Delivery Tracking (Optional Enhancement)

* Delivery agent updates status (if integrated)
* Location updates may be recorded
* Buyer receives real-time notifications

---

### 📦 Step 4: Delivery Confirmation

Once the item reaches the buyer, confirmation is required.

There are two validation methods:

---

#### Option A: Buyer Confirmation

1. Buyer clicks **“Confirm Delivery”**
2. System updates status to:

```text id="status3"
DELIVERED (PENDING VERIFICATION)
```

---

#### Option B: OTP / Delivery Agent Verification (Recommended)

1. Delivery agent presents OTP generated by PayBridge
2. Buyer provides OTP to agent OR enters it in-app
3. System validates OTP
4. Delivery is confirmed

---

### 🟢 Step 5: Escrow Release

Once delivery is confirmed:

1. Status updates to:

```text id="status4"
DELIVERED & VERIFIED
```

2. Escrow system triggers fund release
3. Funds are transferred to seller’s account
4. Seller receives payout notification

---

### 🔁 Transaction Lifecycle Summary

```text id="flow1"
INVOICE CREATED
   ↓
PAYMENT COMPLETED
   ↓
FUNDS IN ESCROW
   ↓
IN TRANSIT
   ↓
DELIVERED (CONFIRMATION REQUIRED)
   ↓
VERIFIED DELIVERY
   ↓
FUNDS RELEASED
```

## Monetization Model

PayBridge uses a transparent escrow fee system.

---

### 💰 Total Platform Fee

* Fixed: **2.5% per transaction**
* Covers:

  * Escrow service
  * Payment processing
  * Platform operations

---

## Fee Responsibility Model

The seller selects how the fee is handled during invoice creation,probably based on agreement wih buyer, or fee is included in the total cost price.

---

### 🟢 Option 1: Buyer Pays Fee

```text id="fee1"
Item Price: ₦100,000  
Fee (2.5%): ₦2,500  

Buyer Pays: ₦102,500  
Seller Receives: ₦100,000  
Platform Fee: ₦2,500  
```

---

### 🔵 Option 2: Seller Pays Fee

```text id="fee2"
Item Price: ₦100,000  
Fee (2.5%): ₦2,500  

Buyer Pays: ₦100,000  
Seller Receives: ₦97,500  
Platform Fee: ₦2,500  
```

---

### 🟡 Option 3: Split Fee

```text id="fee3"
Item Price: ₦100,000  
Fee (2.5%): ₦2,500  

Buyer Pays: ₦101,250  
Seller Receives: ₦98,750  
Platform Fee: ₦2,500  
```
